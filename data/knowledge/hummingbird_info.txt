What is Hummingbird?
Hummingbird is a network for growth, insights, and support. It tokenizes the data intelligence game through AI agents that tap into social media engagement, focus group feedback, and crypto wallet analytics. It creates a vibrant, value-packed data network where community is a superpower.

Core Features:
1. Social Campaigns: Get paid to leverage your network and create real engagement on social media.
2. B2B Campaigns: Tap into the power of the network effect to promote your products or services.
3. Rewards: Users earn tokens, points, or vouchers for completing campaigns for businesses.
4. Staking: The amount of tokens staked is part of the rewards calculation. The security of the platform is greater the more tokens are staked.

The $FLY Token:
The app is powered by the $FLY token, which is used to distribute rewards to the community. It grants access, secures the network, and gives holders a stake in the ecosystem.

KYC and KYB:
Hummingbird requires KYC (Know Your Customer) and KYB (Know Your Business). This is to protect the validity of the network and prevent bot accounts from earning the rewards.
