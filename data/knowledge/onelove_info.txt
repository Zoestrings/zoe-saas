WHAT IS ONELOVE?
$ONELOVE is a Solana community coin built around a real-world community: the One Love Sailing family in the Exumas, Bahamas. It is not a pump-and-dump meme coin; it is a long-term experiment focused on community, transparency, and steady organic growth. The philosophy is: "One Love. One Crew. One Mission."

THE STORY & PHILOSOPHY
- Built on real people and the existing One Love family.
- A public creator, a real business, and an open approach.
