What is Zoe?
Zoe is the central autonomous Web3 intelligence and community management platform. Zoe provides real-time multi-chain token scanning, security audits, DEX liquidity analytics, and automated community moderation.

Core Capabilities:
1. Universal Token Scanner: Detects Solana and EVM contract addresses, fetching live market metrics, liquidity pairs, and price analysis via DexScreener.
2. Security Audits: On-chain contract auditing, honeypot detection, liquidity lock verification, and ownership analysis via GoPlus Security.
3. Multi-Tenant Intelligence: Central coordinator managing sub-bots and community knowledge across diverse Web3 ecosystems.
